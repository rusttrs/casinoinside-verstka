Mirror Joker is a new slot from the popular joker series that features a classic design with 3 reels, 3 rows and 5 fixed paylines that can be expanded to 10 lines during the bonus feature. The theme of the game combines familiar fruit symbols and classic joker elements.

The main feature of the slot is the Mirror Respin function. It is activated when two or three stacks of identical symbols appear on the reels. In this case, the game grid expands to 5 reels, the symbols are mirrored, and the chance of collecting winning combinations increases. During this function, the joker becomes the Mirror Joker symbol, which is a wild and pays in both directions, which increases the likelihood of big wins. Online casinos could lower the RTP of their games by adjusting the algorithms and settings of their games. They could also hide the RTP by not disclosing the information to players, or by making it difficult to access.

Return to Player (RTP) is a crucial metric for players to assess the fairness of an online casino game. It refers to the percentage of total bets that a player can expect to receive back over a long period of play. For example, if the RTP of a game is 96%, players bet $1, that means that the casino earns 4% on every bet.

Online casinos have an incentive to lower the RTP of their games to increase their profits. Lowering the RTP means that the casino will pay out less to players over time, which translates to more profits for the casino.
